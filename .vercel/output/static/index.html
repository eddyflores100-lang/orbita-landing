USA Compliance

Built for the US legal framework

Órbita complies with the Fair Housing Act, RESPA, TRID, the NAR Code of Ethics and MLS rules by design — not bolted on. It is baked into every layer of the product so bilingual agents can operate without legal risk.

FHA

Fair Housing Act

Prohibits discrimination in housing based on 7 protected classes: race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, disability.

Key rules
  • •No demographic language in property descriptions (e.g., 'family neighborhood', 'perfect for young couple')
  • •No references to schools by demographic composition
  • •No steering (directing buyers to/away from areas based on protected class)

Órbita's AI Director uses 'family-neutral' language. Photo subjects filtered to exclude people (room-only). Fair Housing-compliant tone profiles (luxury, casual, boutique — never demographic).

RESPA

Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act

Section 8 prohibits kickbacks and referral fees for settlement services. Marketing services agreements (MSAs) must have documented fair market value.

Key rules
  • •No paid referrals between agents, lenders, title companies, escrow
  • •Marketing Services Agreements must be in writing + documented FMV
  • •Affiliated business arrangements require disclosure

Órbita's pricing is per-property, transparent, and documented. No referral fees, no kickbacks. Marketing services agreement template provided for broker-Órbita partnerships.

TRID

TILA-RESPA Integrated Disclosure

Federal rule requiring combined Loan Estimate + Closing Disclosure forms. Marketing materials cannot display binding loan terms.

Key rules
  • •No binding APR, monthly payment, or loan terms in marketing copy
  • •No specific lender or loan product endorsements in property listings
  • •Generic mortgage calculators allowed with disclaimer

Órbita microsites include generic mortgage calculator with disclaimer: 'Estimated only. Actual terms vary by lender. Not a commitment to lend.' No specific lender tie-ins.

NAR

National Association of Realtors

Trademark 'Realtor' and 'Realtors' is members-only. Code of Ethics requires honesty in advertising.

Key rules
  • •Only NAR members may use 'Realtor' designation
  • •Marketing must not imply discrimination, misrepresentation
  • •Property disclosures required (material defects, lead paint pre-1978)

Órbita watermarks are agent/brokerage-branded only — no 'Realtor' trademark usage without verification. AI-generated content marked with disclosure when applicable.

MLS

MLS Rules (IDX/VOW/Photo Licensing)

Each MLS has rules on listing data display, photo ownership, syndication, refresh rates.

Key rules
  • •Listing broker attribution required on all displays
  • •Sold data only via VOW (Virtual Office Website)
  • •Photos copyrighted by photographer or broker — agents cannot reuse without license

Órbita only accepts broker-owned/licensed media — never scrapes third-party MLS photos. License-back flow: AI-generated derivatives licensed back to broker. Listing broker attribution in all microsites. Daily refresh via MLS Grid RESO Web API.

10-phase pipeline

From photos to a professional listing
in 15 minutes

Each phase is independent, cached and resumable. If something fails, there is a deterministic fallback. If you re-render, cached clips come back instantly. Everything runs on your server or on Órbita Cloud.

01 · INGEST

Upload your photos

Drag-and-drop, ZIP or paste a URL. Any format (JPG/PNG/WebP/HEIC/AVIF). Automatic EXIF rotation, sha-256 dedupe, thumbnails at 640px.

02 · ANALYZE

AI understands the property

GLM-4.5v identifies 15 room types, objects, lighting and architectural style. Generates an automatic description for the listing.

03 · DIRECT

AI Director plans it

LLM decides shot order, 11 camera moves, durations (1.4-6s per clip), captions and narrative tone. You can edit any of it.

04 · RENDER

CogVideoX-3 generates video

Each photo → 5s cinematic clip. CogVideoX image-to-video with auto-built prompt (place + camera + atmosphere + quality guards).

05 · MONTAGE

ffmpeg xfade montage

Clips concatenated with 0.55s crossfade. Reliable fallback to concat copy if xfade fails. Normalized to 30fps yuv420p +faststart.

06 · MUSIC

Procedural PCM

16-bit stereo WAV synthesis with 6 styles (cinematic, ambient, uplifting, minimal, warm, epic). Chord progressions, ADSR, arpeggio panning. No third-party licenses.

07 · VOICEOVER

TTS + sidechain duck

LLM writes a script calibrated to 2.6 words/sec. TTS with 4 voices. ffmpeg sidechaincompress for real ducking (music dips under voice).

08 · 3D TOUR

Depth Anything V2

Three.js point cloud with monocular depth via 94MB ONNX. Drag-to-look, wheel/pinch, mobile gyroscope. Labeled hotspots up to 24KB.

09 · MICROSITE

Publish + QR + WhatsApp

SSR microsite at /p/[slug] with hero, video, 3D, gallery, WhatsApp CTA, contact form. Dynamic 640px QR PNG with ?ref=qr for attribution.

10 · ANALYTICS

14 days of tracking

Events: VIEW, VIDEO_PLAY, CTA, WHATSAPP, SCAN, CONTACT. Daily views + byRef (qr/instagram/whatsapp) + totals. Per property.
