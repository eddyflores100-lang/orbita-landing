# Órbita pilot operation

## Offer and acceptance

Start with one property, a usable set of photos and one approved objective. Quote the exact video length/aspect ratio, microsite content, hosting/domain responsibility, revision count, delivery date, usage rights and payment milestones. Review sample input before accepting the job; do not sell a fixed automated SLA.

Before delivery, verify: video opens on mobile/desktop, approved facts match the customer's source, contact link reaches the chosen recipient, assets load, and the customer accepts the agreed revision. Record actual production time and external costs to evaluate margin. Do not promise demand, sales or legal compliance as an output of a video tool.

## Boundaries

This repository provides presentation and inquiry, not fulfillment. The video sample is an existing local file, not proof of live AI generation, reconstruction accuracy or property availability. City pages do not establish local offices or coverage. No MLS credentials or integrations exist here. Review property media rights before public promotion.

Email preparation stays in browser memory. Sending, response handling, invoicing and delivery are manual. Confirm contact@alicelabs.site receives inquiries before directing traffic; do not collect property files through fake upload controls.

## Launch sequence

1. Review the exported pilot and the revised factual privacy/conditions wording.
2. Confirm sample media publication rights and the contact inbox.
3. Confirm the hostname, Pages project and Cloudflare account; set repository secrets and public URL variable.
4. Publish the validated artifact through the manual production workflow; check homepage, sample video, inquiry email and legacy paths.
5. Keep previous deployment rollback available. Monitor real inquiries and fulfilled jobs, not invented conversion metrics.

No production deployment was performed during this restructuring. The prior deployment failure was missing credentials, not a failed compile. CI verification is intentionally independent of Cloudflare access.
